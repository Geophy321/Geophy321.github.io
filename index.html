About

I'm a geophysicist based in Stockholm, currently working as a Specialist in Infrastructure Hazard Monitoring at HydroResearch Solutions AB, where I help run fibre-optic monitoring (DTS, DSS, DAS) across embankment dams — from field deployment through to interpretation and technical reporting. Before that I held postdoctoral positions in Brazil, Belgium, Italy, and the United States, working on ambient-noise seismology, near-surface geophysics, and karst hydrogeophysics. I hold a Ph.D. in Geotechnical Engineering from the University of Brasília, and I currently serve as Handling Editor at Seismica and Associate Editor at IJEEG.
