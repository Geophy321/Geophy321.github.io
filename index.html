Yawar Hussain

Geophysicist ( Fibre-Optic Sensing / Seismology / Geohazards )

Work

Yawar is a geophysicist based in Stockholm with a background spanning near-surface geophysics, seismic monitoring and geohazard characterisation, built over postdoctoral appointments in Brazil, Belgium, Italy and the United States. He currently works as a Specialist in Infrastructure Hazard Monitoring, supporting fibre-optic monitoring (DTS, DSS, DAS) of dams and embankments from field deployment through to interpretation. Over 35 peer-reviewed publications, an H-index of 19 and 1284 citations (Scopus).

Employment

Specialist Infrastructure Hazard Monitoring (01/2026 to Present)
HydroResearch Solutions AB, Stockholm, Sweden. Supporting the expansion of fibre-optic monitoring technologies (DTS, DSS, DAS) through integrated field measurements, system operation, and data evaluation. Research Associate (11/2024 to 06/2025)
Department of Sustainable Earth Systems Sciences, University of Texas at Dallas, USA. Engaged in near-surface geophysics and modeling to study the Pecos Valley Aquifer System in Texas. Worked with various versions of the PulseEKKO GPR series, configuring them for both field operations and instructional demonstrations. Department of Earth Science, 'Sapienza' University of Rome, Italy. Analysis of data from multiparametric monitoring systems for detection of slope instabilities affecting strategic infrastructures and cultural heritage (supervision: Dr. S. Martino).
