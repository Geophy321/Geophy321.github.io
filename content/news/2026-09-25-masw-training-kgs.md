Title: Completed MASW Training with the Kansas Geological Survey
Date: 2026-09-27
Summary: Completed a Kansas Geological Survey course on MASW software, case histories and technique adaptation.
Cover: images/masw-training-1.png

I recently completed a training course on Multichannel Analysis of Surface Waves (MASW), run by the Kansas Geological Survey. It covered the MASW software itself, a range of case histories from different regions, and how to adapt the method to different site conditions. About 16 of us took part, from different parts of the world.

![MASW training session](../../images/news_b1.JPG)

I already knew most of the core techniques, but the course was still valuable — it's a good platform to see how other practitioners around the world approach the same problems, especially since interpretation in MASW is often fairly subjective. The case studies were presented with real data as hands-on exercises, which made the software side much more concrete.

![MASW software walkthrough](../../images/news_b2.JPG)

This ties in well with where my work is heading. My company is expanding into geophysical measurements, and I'm hoping to apply MASW, alongside DAS, to dam sites in Sweden that are dealing with cavity evolution and piping in the bedrock. Good course, and a good reminder that there's always more than one way to read the same data.
