Summary: A quick note on the posting workflow — delete once real posts are up.

Posts live as Markdown files in `content/posts/`. Each file needs three
metadata lines at the top (Title, Date, Summary) followed by the post body
in normal Markdown.

The newest posts show first on the Blog page and the most recent one gets
a teaser on the homepage. No other file needs to change — just add a new
`.md` file and push to `main`.
